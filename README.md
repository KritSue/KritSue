## Featured projects

### Project A — SAG Mill Condition Monitoring

An in-progress Master of Engineering research project investigating vibration-based monitoring on a laboratory SAG mill. The work focuses on trustworthy measurement, repeatable experiments, and careful analysis of vibration and rotational references.

- **Project website:** [SAG Mill Online Health Monitoring](https://kritsue.github.io/2026s2-EME-LCh-PG-81158/)
- **Repository:** [2026s2-EME-LCh-PG-81158](https://github.com/KritSue/2026s2-EME-LCh-PG-81158) *(repository access may be restricted)*
- **Research areas:** accelerometer measurements, time-domain analysis, FFT, Welch PSD, STFT/spectrogram methods where appropriate, rotational-reference verification, repeatability, and liner-condition investigation
- **Tools and methods:** MATLAB, NI DAQ, LabVIEW, vibration measurement, and signal processing
- **Measurement boundary:** A0 is vertical and A1 horizontal; current signals are in volts, not calibrated acceleration. DAQ timing, sensor calibration, encoder/RPM mapping, and condition ground truth remain under verification. No validated liner classification or particle-size prediction is claimed.
